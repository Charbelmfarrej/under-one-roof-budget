Under One Roof: Budget

A shared household finance app for couples, families and flatmates. Everyone at home sees the same entries, accounts and budgets.

Important: Under One Roof: Budget is a visualisation of your household's finances based on what you enter. It is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and it does not connect to your bank or move money.
