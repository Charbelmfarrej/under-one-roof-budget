Common questions

How do I invite someone to my household?

Create an account in the app, then open More and share your household's invite code. The other person installs the app, creates their own account and enters the code. A household has up to 6 people.

Why should I create an account?

Without an account your data lives only on your phone and is lost if you lose or reset the phone. With an account your household data is also stored online, so it comes back on a new phone and can be shared with the people you live with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by us. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Under One Roof: Budget → Cancel. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel the subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Log in to your account and tap Restore purchases on the plan screen. Use the same Apple ID or Google account that bought the plan.

How do I delete my account and data?

See Delete your account and data.

Important information

Under One Roof: Budget is a visualisation of your household's finances. It does not give financial advice, does not connect to your bank and does not move money. Portfolio prices are entered by you and may be out of date.
