Delete your account and data

In the app (fastest)

Open More, scroll to Account, tap Delete account and confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

By email, if you cannot open the app

Send an email to underoneroof.support@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account, from the email address you used to sign up. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email address and password).
  • Your link to the household and, if you are the last person in the household, all of the household's entries, accounts, budgets and other data stored online.
  • Your subscription record in our system.

What stays

  • Shared household data stays for the other members. If other people are still in your household, the entries and accounts they share remain available to them. Entries you created stay as part of the household's records.
  • Data on your phone (including receipt photos, which are never uploaded) stays until you delete the app or use Start over.
  • Your subscription is not cancelled. Apple or Google bill you, and only you can cancel in the App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
  • Billing records held by Apple, Google and our subscription service RevenueCat follow their own retention rules.
