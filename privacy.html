Privacy policy

Under One Roof: Budget · last updated 21 October 2026

Who is responsible

Charbel Mfarrej, Klosterweg 20, 8913 Ottenbach, Switzerland · underoneroof.support@gmail.com. Swiss data protection law (revFADP) applies and, for people in the EU, the GDPR.

The short version. Without an account, everything stays on your phone and we receive nothing. With an account, your household's entries are stored online so they can be shared with the people you invite. We do not sell data, show ads, track you or use analytics. The app does not connect to your bank.

Without an account

You can use the app without creating an account. Entries, accounts, categories, budgets, members, saving pots, portfolio positions and settings are then stored only on your phone. We cannot see them. If you delete the app or lose the phone, this data is lost unless you made a backup.

With an account

When you create an account, we collect:

DataWhy
Email address and password (stored only as a protected hash)To log you in and let you recover your account
Your household data: entries and amounts, accounts and balances, categories, budgets, repeating payments, favourites, saving pots, portfolio positions you type in, member names and emoji, settingsTo keep your data safe if you change phone and to share it live with the people in your household
Your household membership and invite codeTo decide who may see the household's data
Subscription statusTo tell the app whether the household's plan is active

Household data is visible only to signed-in members of that household. You can be a member of one household at a time. Anyone you invite can see everything in the household, so only share the invite code with people you trust.

Photos stay on your phone. Receipt photos and profile photos are stored only on your phone and are not uploaded.

Where your data is stored

Account and household data is stored with our database provider Supabase, on servers in Ireland (European Union). Data is encrypted in transit.

Other services the app contacts

  • Exchange rates: when you add an entry in a foreign currency, the app asks frankfurter.dev (European Central Bank rates) for the rate. The request contains the two currency codes and, as with any web request, your IP address.
  • Security names: if you type an ISIN in the portfolio, the app sends that ISIN to OpenFIGI (Bloomberg's open data service) to look up the name.
  • Purchases: subscriptions are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play and by our subscription service provider RevenueCat. They process your purchase, an app user ID (the ID of your account) and your IP address so the app knows whether your plan is active. We never see your payment details.

Some of these providers are located outside Switzerland and the EU, for example in the United States. They are bound by contracts or recognised safeguards for such transfers.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no analytics about you.
  • No selling or renting of data.
  • No bank connection. Bank statements you import are read on your phone only.

Permissions

  • Camera and photos: only when you tap the camera button to attach a receipt. The photo stays on your phone.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on the daily reminder. It is a local reminder set on your phone, with no data sent to a server.

How long we keep data

Account and household data is kept until you delete your account (see below). If other people remain in a household when you leave, the shared household data stays available to them. Entries marked as deleted are removed from our servers after 30 days.

Your rights and deleting your account

You can ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and receive a copy. In the app you can delete your account yourself (More → Account → Delete account), export your data as a backup file, and delete everything on your phone with "Start over". See Delete your account and data. For other requests write to underoneroof.support@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or your local authority.

Not financial advice

Under One Roof: Budget is a visualisation of your household's finances based on what you enter. It does not give financial, investment, tax or legal advice and does not move money. Portfolio prices are entered by you and may be out of date.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If we change how data is used, we update this page and the date above before the change goes live.
